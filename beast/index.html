Why Beast Industries?

What draws me to Beast Industries is the engineering challenge behind creating unforgettable experiences for millions of people. At Aerospace, I work on systems where latency, throughput and reliability are fundamental requirements. I want to apply that same engineering discipline to live entertainment, where a delayed update or inconsistent game state directly affects the fan experience. My background in technology and psychology gives me a perspective that extends beyond the backend: I care about what users feel when they interact with a product. This interactive portfolio is my way of demonstrating how I approach an unfamiliar product space, translate requirements into a working prototype, and iterate using AI-assisted development. I’m excited by the opportunity to build from zero to one, take ownership across the stack, and turn ambitious ideas into experiences people genuinely want to use.
